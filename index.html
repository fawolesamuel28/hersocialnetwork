Our purpose

We believe every woman deserves access to opportunities that build confidence, develop practical skills and improve wellbeing.

We provide accessible programmes that help women overcome barriers to employment, education and entrepreneurship.

We create a safe, nourishing space where women can learn, grow and thrive together.

About our organisation

Founded for the women of Luton and Bedfordshire

Her Social Network CIC was established to improve the lives of women across Luton and Bedfordshire by creating opportunities for personal growth, economic independence and community connection. Our mission is to provide accessible programmes that help women overcome barriers to employment, education and entrepreneurship while creating a supportive network where they can learn, grow and thrive.

Our mission

To provide accessible programmes that help women overcome barriers to employment, education and entrepreneurship, in a safe, nourishing environment where women can nurture each other, learn and grow together.

Our vision

To become a trusted community organisation that equips women with the confidence, skills and support needed to improve their lives, strengthen their families and contribute positively to their local communities.
